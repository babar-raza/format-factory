## Core Correction

Machinery (supervisor, acceleration, skills, autonomous continuation) is only useful if it helps achieve the POC goals faster. Machinery that audits itself, repairs its own evidence, or generates prompt-quality metrics without producing product output is drift.

## Corrected Lane Definitions

### 1. Mainstream Product
Product output engine. Must produce real capability breadth across commercial and FOSS products. Cannot pass with evidence repair alone.

### 2. Acceleration
Two sub-lanes:
- **Acceleration-A:** Governance acceleration / anti-skip / prompt-quality / evidence-quality safety harness.
- **Acceleration-B:** AI product acceleration / LLM / embeddings / retrieval / spec understanding / source-pattern mining / code-generation handoffs / test generation / product gap ranking.

Acceleration must prove it makes Mainstream faster, safer, or less blocked.

### 3. Skills / Governed Execution
Reusable execution skills, handoffs, transcripts, receiver fixtures, validation. Must make product source changes faster and safer. Should not produce proof in isolation only.

### 4. Supervisor / Autonomous Continuation
Autonomous traffic controller. Must decide what continues, what stops, what downgrades, whether machinery improves product velocity. Must prevent false PASS and false STOP, route blockers, protect product throughput. Not merely an evidence auditor.

## Acceleration Drift and Correction

Acceleration originally meant AI/LLM/embeddings/retrieval/product-code-generation acceleration. It drifted into anti-skip/prompt-quality machinery. The correction splits it into A (governance harness) and B (AI product acceleration). Both must demonstrate product throughput improvement.

## Autonomous Supervisor Role Correction

The supervisor should not merely audit evidence. It must:
- Decide what continues and what stops
- Downgrade items that overclaim
- Route blockers to the right stream
- Protect product throughput
- Prevent false PASS and false STOP

## Mainstream Product-Output Floor

No machinery lane may declare clean success unless it either:
- Removes a blocker for Mainstream product work
- Prevents a false PASS or false STOP that affects product velocity
- Creates a reusable accelerator that Mainstream can consume
- Reduces human handoff
- Improves product throughput, safety, or repeatability
