### Closure Mechanism Assessment

The gap ledger uses an **automated closure model**: the `autonomous_task_generator.py` system scans for test coverage and marks gaps as `closed` when capabilities have test coverage detected. This is a systematic, repeatable process — not arbitrary.

**Evidence that closures are plausible (not fabricated):**
- 98.7% of closed gaps are `missing_test_coverage` type — consistent with an automated scan
- The `product-task-candidates.json` file shows only 1 unclosed gap (GAP-TOML-FOSS-INSTALLED_WO-001) — the remainder were detectably covered
- `closed_at` timestamps on 51% of entries follow a realistic distribution (majority in 2026-06)
- Top closed formats (FODT=123, XCF=88, FODS=82) align with where the most development work happened

**Evidence that quality is limited:**
- No `closed_by` field linking any closure to a specific sprint, evidence bundle, or commit
- 49% of closed entries have no `closed_at` timestamp
- Only 6 entries (0.5%) have `closure_evidence` — the gold-standard proof
- The closure model does not distinguish "test exists" from "test passes"

---

### Verdict

**INDETERMINATE**

The 96.9% closure rate is likely **plausible** but **not verifiable** from the ledger alone. Closures appear to be systematically generated by capability scanning (not fabricated), but the ledger lacks direct evidence linking each closure to passing tests, sprints, or evidence bundles. Verifying the closure rate would require cross-referencing 1,203 gap IDs against actual passing test files — which is outside the scope of this audit.

### Reasoning

The 98.7% concentration on `missing_test_coverage` gaps, combined with automated closure mechanics in `autonomous_task_generator.py`, makes it likely that these gaps represent real test coverage additions. However, without `closed_by` sprint references or `closure_evidence` fields on the "closed" entries, the audit cannot confirm each closure is backed by a genuinely passing test. The 6 `test_verified` entries with full evidence quality should be the baseline for all future closures.

### Next Action

The verdict is INDETERMINATE (not OVERCLAIMED), so no TC-LA-GAP-REOPEN-001 taskcard is required. Recommendation for future sprints:

1. Require `closed_by` sprint reference on all new closures
2. Promote `test_verified` as the only valid final closure status (not `closed`)
3. The 30 `DEFERRED_BY_DESIGN` entries are appropriate and do not indicate overclaiming
