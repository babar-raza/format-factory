Introduction
This document demonstrates the FODT TXT export via FormatFactory.Txt.TxtWriter.
The exporter reads all paragraphs from the FODT body and delegates plaintext output to the writer library.

Section 1
Each paragraph becomes a line in the output.
Headings are written as plain text without markup.

Conclusion
The TxtWriter normalises line endings to LF and writes UTF-8 without BOM.
