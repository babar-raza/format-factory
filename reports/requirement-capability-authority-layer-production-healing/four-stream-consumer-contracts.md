# Four-Stream and Additional Consumer Contracts

Sprint ID: FORMAT-FACTORY-REQUIREMENT-CAPABILITY-AUTHORITY-LAYER-PRODUCTION-BLOCKER-HEALING-001
Owner: Lane D

## Contract 1: Mainstream

### Consumes
- mainstream-gap-queue.json (from MainstreamGapQueueGenerator, via Supervisor)
- Required claim IDs and expected_files/expected_tests/expected_dogfood per gap entry
- capability-family-model for target-specific proof sufficiency requirements

### Produces
- CapabilityDelta proposal documents (capability-delta-proposal-template filled out)
- Source file implementations (ImplementationArtifact candidates)
- Test files (TestArtifact candidates)
- Dogfood output files (DogfoodArtifact candidates)
- EvidencePackage (evidence declaration + materialized ZIP)
- Phrased as: "here is evidence to promote this claim" — not "this claim is now accepted"

### Cannot Do
- Cannot directly accept a CapabilityClaim (no claim state mutation without delta+evaluator)
- Cannot mutate poc-targets.yaml directly
- Cannot produce a SupervisorVerdictPacket
- Cannot assert PASS without CapabilityCoverageEvaluator confirmation
- Cannot skip the CapabilityDelta schema validation step

### Authority Boundary
- Mainstream is a contributor of evidence only
- Mainstream proposes; evaluator validates; Supervisor decides
- Any Mainstream sprint report that says "claim X is now PASS" without a CapabilityDelta + CoverageRecord is an authority violation

## Contract 2: Skills

### Consumes
- ProductRequirement IDs and CapabilityClaim IDs for the governed operation
- Capability family model for the format being handled
- Sprint context: what the handoff is for and what evidence it must produce

### Produces
- Governed handoff documents with required_claim_ids field populated
- Transcript artifacts (skills transcripts referencing claim IDs)
- Delta template stubs (pre-filled capability-delta-proposal-template ready for Mainstream to complete)
- StreamHandoff nodes importable by EvidenceGraphImporter

### Cannot Do
- Cannot accept capability claims
- Cannot produce CapabilityCoverageEvaluator results
- Cannot mutate any authority registry
- Cannot assert that a capability is proven based on the handoff alone

### Authority Boundary
- Skills is a governed execution layer; it produces structured handoffs, not authority updates
- Skills transcripts and handoffs are candidate EvidenceArtifact records until graph-linked to specific claims

---

## Contract 3: Acceleration

### Consumes
- Proof graph summaries (source_graph_hash, stale_claims, overclaim_risks)
- Current mainstream-gap-queue.json for context
- Capability family model (for format context)

### Produces
- ai_draft recommendations (advisory only; never proof)
- Gap analysis suggestions (advisory)
- Candidate CapabilityDelta stubs labeled ai_draft=true
- Risk assessment summaries (advisory)

### Cannot Do
- Cannot satisfy any proof class requirement (ai_draft nodes are excluded from proof evaluation)
- Cannot produce a SupervisorVerdictPacket
- Cannot accept or reject capability claims
- Cannot mutate poc-targets.yaml
- Cannot assert PASS for any capability

### Authority Boundary
- All Acceleration outputs are advisory
- Any Acceleration output used as proof is an authority violation
- ai_draft=true is set on all Acceleration-produced nodes; these nodes are never traversed in CapabilityCoverageEvaluator

---

## Contract 4: Supervisor

### Consumes
- supervisor-verdict-packet.json (generated by SupervisorVerdictPacketGenerator)
- Machine-readable fields: claims_checked, coverage_records, poc_readiness_verdict, overclaim_risks, stale_claims, recommended_supervisor_decision

### Produces
- Accepted or rejected CapabilityDelta records
- Authorized PocTargetsSyncProposalGenerator invocations
- Sprint authorization and AUTONOMOUS_CONTINUE decision
- Next sprint prompt (next-sprint.md) grounded in gap queue

### Cannot Do
- Cannot infer PASS from prose report patterns alone
- Cannot accept a CapabilityDelta without coverage_computed=PASS from the evaluator
- Cannot directly mutate poc-targets.yaml
- Cannot ignore false_pass_risks and false_stop_risks fields in the verdict packet

### Authority Boundary
- Supervisor is the final decision authority for claim acceptance
- Supervisor decision is only as good as the verdict packet; heterogeneous prose must not substitute for the packet
- Supervisor may override recommended_supervisor_decision with documented justification

---

## Contract 5: Specification Authority

### Consumes
- Formal specification documents (ODF spec, empirical format samples, technical documentation)
- ProductPolicyDecision requests from Mainstream or Supervisor
- Context pack snapshots for cross-sprint reference

### Produces
- Accepted SpecRequirementRef nodes (spec_name, version, section)
- Accepted EmpiricalEvidence nodes (with caveat when no formal spec)
- ProductPolicyDecision records (with human decision_id)
- ContextPackRef nodes (context_pack_id, graph_hash, generated_at)
- Staleness notifications when spec versions change

### Cannot Do
- Cannot accept CapabilityClaims
- Cannot produce CoverageRecords
- Cannot assert POC readiness
- Context pack alone does not prove capability; it informs context only

### Authority Boundary
- Specification Authority is the source of ProductRequirement backing only
- ContextPackRef informs but does not prove; every RequirementProof must link to a SpecRequirementRef, EmpiricalEvidence, or ProductPolicyDecision — not just a ContextPackRef

---

## Contract 6: Evidence

### Consumes
- Declared artifact paths from evidence-declaration.yaml
- CapabilityClaim IDs that each artifact supports
- EvidencePackage manifest entries

### Produces
- Materialized EvidencePackage ZIPs with verified checksums
- EvidenceArtifact nodes (TestArtifact, DogfoodArtifact, ExampleArtifact, ImplementationArtifact) linked to claims
- ImportConflict records where artifact paths cannot resolve
- declared_not_verified flags for unverifiable manifest entries

### Cannot Do
- Cannot promote a CapabilityClaim to accepted_for_poc
- Cannot produce CoverageRecords
- Cannot assert that file existence proves capability truth

### Authority Boundary
- Evidence proves only the artifacts it includes, when: (a) checksums match declared values, (b) artifacts are graph-linked to CapabilityClaims via correct edge types, (c) materialized=true
- EvidencePackage is a necessary but not sufficient condition for coverage_validated
