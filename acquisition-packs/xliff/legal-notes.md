# Legal Notes: XLIFF

## Classification
- **Legal Category:** 1
- **Category Name:** Open Standard RF
- **Patent Status:** Royalty-free (OASIS IPR Policy)
- **License:** OASIS RF on RAND Terms

## Implementation Freedom
- Read: Unrestricted
- Write: Unrestricted
- Distribution: Unrestricted under Apache 2.0

## Notes
XLIFF is an OASIS standard published under their Royalty-Free on RAND Terms IPR policy. All OASIS TC members have made RF commitments for essential claims. The standard has been widely adopted in the localization industry with many independent implementations (Okapi Framework, Memsource, SDL Trados, memoQ). No known patent litigation. Safe for parser implementation under the OASIS RF policy.
