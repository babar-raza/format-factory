## OASIS Royalty-Free License Analysis

The ODF 1.3 specification is published by OASIS under their RF (Royalty-Free) IPR Policy
on Limited Terms. Key provisions:
- Implementation of the specification is royalty-free
- No patent claims by OASIS members that would block implementation
- Redistribution of the spec document itself is not permitted
- Implementation output (parsers, converters) is not restricted

This is the same legal basis as FODS and FODT (legal_category: 1), which have already
passed Gate 2 legal review.

## FODP-Specific Legal Notes

- Aspose.Slides support for FODP (LoadFormat.Fodp, SaveFormat.Fodp) is commercially licensed

- No patent thicket identified for XML-based ODF flat presentation format
