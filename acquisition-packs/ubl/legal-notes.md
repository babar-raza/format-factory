# Legal Notes: OASIS Universal Business Language

## Classification
- **Legal Category:** 1
- **Category Name:** Open Standard RF
- **Patent Status:** Royalty-free (OASIS IPR Policy)
- **License:** OASIS RF on RAND Terms

## Implementation Freedom
- Read: Unrestricted
- Write: Unrestricted
- Distribution: Unrestricted under Apache 2.0

## Notes
UBL is an OASIS standard published under their Royalty-Free on RAND Terms IPR policy. It is mandated by the European Union for e-invoicing (EN 16931) and adopted by governments worldwide. Extensive independent implementations exist across all major programming languages and ERP systems. No known patent encumbrances. The XSD schemas are freely available. Safe for parser implementation under the OASIS RF policy.
